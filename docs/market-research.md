# Market research (October 2026)

Quick desk research on who else serves small businesses, to position Origo. Figures come from secondary sources found via web search and from vendor pricing pages as reported by third-party reviews; **verify on each vendor's own site before quoting them publicly.**

## Who is in this space

| Player | What they do | Reported entry pricing |
|---|---|---|
| Durable | AI website builder with basic CRM and invoicing | Free tier; Launch about $25/mo, Grow about $99/mo |
| Wix (Harmony AI) | Website builder with AI, big app ecosystem | Paid plans from about $17/mo |
| Hostinger | Cheap hosting and AI/website builder | Low intro prices; watch renewal costs |
| Shopify (Sidekick) | Online store platform with AI assistant | About $29-39/mo basic; AI features tied to paid plans |
| Square (Square AI) | POS and payments with AI insights | Free to start; paid tiers about $49 and $149/mo |
| QuickBooks (Intuit Assist) | Accounting with AI features | About $38-275/mo depending on plan |
| Bluehost | Hosting; recently announced AI agents for sites, stores, leads and booking | n/a |
| Bookipi | AI site builder bundled with invoicing, payroll and CRM | n/a |

## What this tells us

1. **AI website builders are crowded.** Building a site from a prompt is no longer a differentiator. We must not compete only there.
2. **Most players own one slice** (site, POS, or accounting). Each adds AI to its own slice.
3. **Nobody is clearly owning "idea to first sale".** The guided path, with learning built in and a coach that sees the whole business, is our opening.
4. **Payments are the economic engine** (Square-style). Low subscriptions can be funded by payment fees.

## Why the problem is real

- Business survival: US BLS data reported by secondary sources puts first-year survival around 80% and five-year survival around 50%.
- Cash flow: multiple secondary sources cite poor cash flow and mismanagement as a leading cause of failure (often quoted as 82%; the original studies should be checked before we use the number in marketing).

## Positioning

> Not another tool. A guided path from idea to first sale, with every tool you need already connected, and an AI coach that sees the whole business.

## Risks to watch

- Large incumbents (Shopify, Wix, Square, Intuit) can ship "launch wizards" quickly. Our edge must be the guided outcome, niche focus and data that compounds, not any single feature.
- Payments need a licensed partner and rules differ by country.
- Do not promise income or guaranteed outcomes in marketing.
